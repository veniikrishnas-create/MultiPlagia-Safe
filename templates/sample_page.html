Step-by-Step Guide to Using PlagiaSafe

Step 1: Login

Enter your username & password to access the plagiarism checker.

Step 2: Select Category

Choose your category (Story, Essay, Logo, Painting, etc.) before uploading.

Step 3: Upload File

Upload your text file (.txt, .docx) or image (.jpg, .png) for checking.

Step 4: Check Plagiarism

Click the “Check Plagiarism” button and wait while your file is scanned.

Step 5: View Results

See plagiarism percentage, matched content highlights, or side-by-side image comparison.

Step 6: Download Report

Download your detailed plagiarism report in PDF/HTML format with date & time.

Step 7: View History

Check your previous plagiarism results and download past reports anytime.
