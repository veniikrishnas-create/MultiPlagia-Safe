💡 PlagiaSafe Tips

1. Document Upload Tips

  • ✅ Always use standard file formats: PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT.
  • ✅ Keep file size manageable to ensure faster processing.
  • ✅ Avoid password-protected or corrupted files as they cannot be scanned.

2. Text & Content Tips

  • 💡 Double-check quotations and citations to reduce false positives.
  • 💡 Use your own words where possible, but keep clarity.
  • 💡 Compare your draft with reference material to catch accidental overlaps.

3. Code & Programming Tips

  • 🐍 Comment your code properly; comments are ignored during plagiarism detection.
  • 🐍 Follow proper indentation and naming conventions to make your code readable.
  • 🐍 Avoid copying entire functions; refactor and optimize instead.

4. Slides & Presentation Tips

  • 🖼️ Use your own diagrams or images where possible.
  • 🖼️ Add references for external content to reduce match highlights.
  • 🖼️ Avoid copying full text slides from the internet; summarize in your own words.

5. Resume & Documents Tips

  • 📑 Tailor your resume; do not copy templates from others entirely.
  • 📑 Keep content factual and up-to-date.
  • 📑 Avoid reusing old submissions word-for-word.

6. Best Practices for PlagiaSafe

  • 🔹 Always review highlighted matches to understand plagiarism sources.
  • 🔹 Use paraphrasing wisely; avoid changing only a few words.
  • 🔹 Keep a backup of your original files before editing.
  • 🔹 Regularly test new types of files to understand detection results.
