About PlagiaSafe

PlagiaSafe is a modern plagiarism detection system designed for students, teachers, and professionals. Our goal is to make content originality checks faster, smarter, and easier to use. Whether you're submitting assignments, publishing research, or creating content, PlagiaSafe ensures that your work maintains academic integrity and credibility.

✨ Key Features

  • Text Checking: Detects duplicate or paraphrased text across multiple sources.
  • Code Comparison: Helps students and developers find copied or reused code blocks.
  • Resume & Document Scan: Ensures originality in professional resumes and documents.
  • Report Generation: Creates detailed reports with similarity percentage and highlighted matches.
  • User History: Keeps track of all your past plagiarism checks for easy access.

🚀 Why Choose PlagiaSafe?

PlagiaSafe isn’t just a plagiarism checker—it’s a platform built with modern tools and an easy-to-use interface. We combine accuracy with simplicity, giving you fast results that you can trust. Our dashboard provides insights, charts, and a history of your scans to keep everything organized.

🌍 Our Mission

Our mission is to support education and originality. By preventing plagiarism, we encourage creativity, honesty, and academic growth. We aim to provide students, educators, and professionals with tools that help them succeed without compromising integrity.
